The Cinder Vault

Click reachable hexes to move or enemy hexes to attack after choosing an action. 🔒 Enter a sealed door to reveal the next room. ✦ Collect the archive seal, defeat the guardian, and reach the exit. Hidden rooms cannot be entered or targeted. Blue = allies; red = enemies; gold = loot; ◆ = obstacles (block walking, not sight); ▰ = walls (block movement and sight); amber = difficult terrain (+1 movement). Allies can be passed through but not landed on. Jump and flying ignore intervening ground obstacles. Targeting requires hex range and a clear corner-to-corner sight line.
